Detailed Description

template<typename T>
class estimators::mvue::MVUE_2< T >

Minimum Variance Unbiased Estimator for two measurements.

The MVUE_2 class combines two measurements into a single estimate while minimizing the variance of the resulting estimate under the assumptions of the estimator.

Each measurement is associated with a variance. The measurement with lower variance receives a greater contribution to the final estimate.

Template Parameters
TNumeric data type used for the estimator.
Example
estimators::mvue::MVUE_2<double> estimator;
estimator.init(var1, var2);
double estimate = estimator.update(x1, x2);
